# Try Denodo: entry from denodo.com

Mockup page: `www/pages/try-denodo-entry-mockup.html` (labelled MOCKUP: it is not the live denodo.com, and its links are for illustration only)

## What the visitor sees

Selecting **Try Denodo** (top navigation) or **Try the Denodo Platform** (hero button) opens a panel on the right of denodo.com. The page behind it is blurred.

### 1. Choose a role

**Get access to a live Denodo environment in a few minutes**

Help Crestbank compare loan repayments from its Gold and Platinum customers and find its most valuable clients for retention. Which role will you play?

Not sure? Which sounds like you:
- I build pipelines and data models → suggests Data Engineer
- I analyse data and build reports → suggests Business Analyst
- I build AI agents and applications → suggests AI Builder

| Role | Card text | Tools | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Data Engineer** | Federates live customer, loan and payment data into a reusable data product, without copying or moving data. | Design Studio, Data Modeling Wizard | About 5 min |
| **Business Analyst** | Uses Data Marketplace and Denodo Assistant to compare repayments by loyalty tier and find the top customers. | Data Marketplace, Denodo Assistant | About 4 min |
| **AI Builder** | Connects AI agents to live banking data through Denodo MCP Server to deliver accurate, up-to-date answers. | AI SDK, MCP Server | About 3 min |

Other ways to try Denodo:
- **Free download**: Install the Denodo Platform trial on your own machine. *Download now*
- **Managed cloud service**: The full Denodo Platform with Agora, our fully managed cloud service. *Start free trial*

### 2. Create your trial account

The chosen tour is shown at the top, with a **Change** link and the three things the visitor will do in it. Form fields: Work email, Full name, Company, Country. Button: **Create my <role> trial**.

### 3. Your trial is being prepared

Progress list: Account created · Denodo Platform environment started · Crestbank sample data connected · <role> tour ready. Button: **Start the <role> tour**, which opens `guided-personas.html?role=analyst|engineer|ai&start=1`.

## Behaviour

- Close with ×, the Esc key or a click outside the panel. Focus stays inside the panel while it is open.
- **Back** returns to the previous view. **Change** returns to the role choice.
- Full-width panel on mobile.
- Sign-up and environment preparation are simulated in the mockup and labelled as such. Nothing is sent.
