How the guided tours work

Every step takes place in the live Denodo Platform loaded with the Crestbank sample data.

01

A guide opens beside Denodo

In Chrome and Edge it stays on top of your other windows. In other browsers it opens as a small window at the side of your screen.

02

Each step opens the right tool

Design Studio, the Data Marketplace or the AI SDK chatbot opens in one browser tab, with a picture of the screen to look for.

03

You confirm each result

Tick a step when you see its result. Your progress is saved in this browser, so you can close the guide and resume later.
